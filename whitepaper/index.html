Abstract

A community network on BNB Smart Chain

BOSS Network is a membership community on BNB Smart Chain (BSC). BOSS stands for Building Opportunities, Skills, and Success, the three things the network is built around.

Members join under a sponsor, deposit USDT and activate tiers. Every activation feeds a published rewards plan (referral, daily, generation and leadership rewards) and also builds a position in the BOSS token, released to the member monthly over five years.

Everything that matters runs in smart contracts on BSC: membership, deposits, tier activation, reward accounting, the reward pool, vesting and the BOSS/USDT market. The rules are the same for every member, and every balance described in this paper can be checked on BscScan.

This paper describes the BOSS token, its tokenomics, the vesting mode, the rewards plan, the market and the contracts, and closes with the roadmap and the risks.

Vesting · 50%

500,000,000 BOSS sit in the vesting contract 0x10E5…D324. This is the members' share: each tier activation reserves BOSS worth the activation for that member, released monthly over 60 months (see Vesting mode). BOSS that no position has reserved stays in the contract.

Locked reserve · 30%

300,000,000 BOSS are set aside as a long-term reserve, to be moved into a dedicated lock contract. That contract is not deployed yet. Until it is, the reserve is held by the deployer wallet 0x2Fd3…1820.

Community · 10%

100,000,000 BOSS fund the community: rewards, airdrops and campaigns, and the BOSS that provides market liquidity in the BOSS/USDT pool on PancakeSwap and in the BOSS Network liquidity contract.

Founders · 10%

100,000,000 BOSS belong to the founders and are to be strategically locked for 5 years, aligning the founders with the long-term network. The lock contract is still to come; until then this share is held by the deployer wallet.

Verify it yourself. Every holder above can be checked on BscScan. The 30% reserve and the 10% founders share are not yet locked on-chain; this paper will be updated with the lock contract addresses once they are deployed.

Vesting mode

How members receive BOSS

  1. Activation creates a position. When a member activates a tier, the vesting contract reserves BOSS worth the activation amount, at the token's market price at that moment.
  2. Released over 60 months. The position is split into 60 equal monthly parts. A new part unlocks every 30 days, counted from the activation date.
  3. Claim any time. One claim collects every unlocked month across all positions. Skipped months are never lost; they add up until claimed.

Example. A $100 activation with BOSS at $0.01 reserves 10,000 BOSS, released as about 166.67 BOSS a month for 60 months.

The vesting contract can only release BOSS it holds. If a position cannot be fully covered, the activation still goes through without a BOSS position; positions are never partial.

Tiers and four ways to earn

A share of each activation by a direct referral: 5% for the first five active directs, 10% from the sixth onward.

Daily1% a day

Every active tier earns 1% of its value each day, collected once every 24 hours.

Generation10% · 10 levels

10% of each daily reward collected by members on your 1st to 10th level.

Leadership pool5% · top 10

5% of every activation goes into a monthly pool, shared by the top 10 sponsors by direct volume.

All rewards need an active tier with capping left, and use the oldest tier's capping first. Withdrawals are 20 to 100 USDT in steps of 20, once every 24 hours, with a 10% fee.

Where daily and generation income come from. They are paid from trading profits that fund the reward pool, never from member deposits. When the pool is short, that part of a withdrawal waits until the pool is funded.

Market

Trading BOSS

  • Pool: BOSS/USDT on PancakeSwap V3, 1% fee tier (0xb0c605eA36577d4cdb47a28bC2FD09651B3225eB).
  • Launch price: 0.01 USDT per BOSS (100 BOSS per USDT). The price is not fixed; it moves with every trade.
  • Buy on the app's Swap page, which routes through the BOSS Network liquidity contract into the pool, or directly on PancakeSwap.
  • Sell on the Swap page through the official PancakeSwap V3 router, into the same pool.
  • Price and chart: CoinMarketCap DEX.

The pool is still small, so large trades move the price. The app simulates every swap before sending it and shows the expected price impact.

Roadmap

Next steps

  1. Reserve lock contract. Deploy the lock contract and move the 300,000,000 BOSS reserve into it.
  2. Founders' 5-year lock. Lock the 100,000,000 BOSS founders share for 5 years on-chain.

This paper will be updated with the lock contract addresses as each step is completed.

Risks and disclaimer

Read before taking part

  • No guaranteed profits. BOSS Network does not promise returns. Daily and generation rewards depend on trading profits funding the reward pool, and trading carries risk of loss.
  • Market risk. The BOSS price can fall as well as rise, and the pool is still small.
  • Smart-contract risk. Contracts can contain bugs despite testing and review.
  • Treasury. A share of each activation goes to the project's trading wallet, and the treasury owner can move USDT to it; withdrawals can wait when the pool or treasury is short.
  • Regulation. A fixed return can be a regulated product in some countries. Get advice for your own jurisdiction.
